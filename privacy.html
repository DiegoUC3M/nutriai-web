Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Summary: NutriAI processes meal-planning inputs and results on your device. NutriAI does not upload those inputs or results. Google AdMob separately processes data for ads, as described below.

1. Introduction

Welcome to NutriAI ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the NutriAI app (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

To generate personalized meal plans, you may optionally provide the following data within the App:

  • Physical data: sex, age, weight, and height (for automatic macro calculations)
  • Activity level and fitness goals (lose, maintain, or gain weight)
  • Food preferences: favorite foods, disliked foods, allergies, and intolerances
  • Meal plan configuration: number of days, meals per day, and variety preferences

Important: Sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and fitness goal are used to calculate macro targets and are not saved as separate fields. The app saves the generated meal plan, macro targets, food preferences and allergy settings, and plan configuration in local storage. No account or registration is required.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

NutriAI does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. The Google AdMob SDK may collect usage, diagnostic, and identifier data as described in Section 4.

3. How We Use Your Information

The personal data you enter into the App (body metrics, food preferences, etc.) is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Calculating your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and macronutrient targets
  • Generating personalized meal plans tailored to your preferences
  • Creating shopping lists based on your generated meal plans
  • Saving your preferences and meal plans locally so they persist between sessions

NutriAI does not send your meal-planning inputs or results to us, to AdMob, or to any other external service.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to serve ads on Android. The web app does not serve AdMob ads. Depending on your device, region, and privacy choices, Google AdMob may collect and share:

  • Device and other identifiers, such as Android advertising ID, app-set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it; NutriAI does not request access to device location
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad and app interactions, such as impressions, clicks, app launches, taps, and video views
  • SDK and app diagnostics, such as crash logs, launch times, hang rates, and energy usage

Google may process this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

At startup, the app checks Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for any required consent message, including in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland. The app requests ads only when UMP indicates that it may do so. When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, select Privacy options above the app's bottom navigation to review or change your ad choices. The planner works without ads. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads or prevent the processing needed to deliver and measure ads where permitted.

5. Data Storage and Security

Saved meal plans and planner settings are stored locally using SharedPreferences (or browser storage on the web). We do not operate servers that store this planner data.

  • Android backups: The app excludes the preferences file containing the saved plan, planner settings, and onboarding status from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Earlier versions may have used different backup rules.
  • Data retention: You can remove the local data by clearing the app's data, uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's browser storage. Backup behavior outside Android can depend on your platform settings.

6. Children's Privacy

NutriAI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require account creation or any form of registration.

NutriAI does not receive the meal-planning inputs or results of any user. AdMob's separate data processing is described in Section 4.

7. Your Rights

  • Access and Deletion: You can view your saved meal plan in the app and delete local planner data as described in Section 5.
  • Ad Preferences: Use Privacy options when it is shown in the app. Android also provides system controls for resetting or deleting your advertising ID. See Google's Privacy Policy for more about Google's data controls.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

If we make significant changes that affect how your data is handled, we will notify users through an update to the App or by posting a prominent notice on this page.
